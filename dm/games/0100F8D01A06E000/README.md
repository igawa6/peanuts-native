# ASTLIBRA Revision (`0100F8D01A06E000`)

Update 1.1.0 (v393216; the base game's files are not needed: the importer stores this update as a complete snapshot). SDK 16.2.0. KEIZO's own engine (WhisperGames publishes it on Switch), no middleware, NVN graphics. Modules `main`, `subsdk0` (GLSLC, the shader compiler) and `sdk`.

**Status (current): plays on Android.** On an AYN Thor (Adreno 740) it boots, passes the title and the menus, and plays the demo's opening areas, with a fight and the menu, at 60 fps with sound, on the system driver and on Turnip V39. It needs the texture pool change of [rendering.md](../../../docs/reference/rendering.md#many-small-descriptor-pools-read-another-pools-textures); without it the title screen, the dialogue box and the HUD draw other pools' textures. Not run on the Mac. The memory layout is the survey's guess.

## Files and shaders

- RomFS: 10,894 files, 1.51 GB. Images in `Image2K/` and `Image720p/` (`.dig`), data in `DAT/` (`.dat`), sound as Ogg, WAV and Opus, movies as `.ogv` and `.mp4`.
- No shader is in any game file: no BNSH or BAHS, and no plain GLSL in the RomFS. The game compiles its shaders at run time with its own GLSLC. The PC split of the files alone finds 0.
- The tables hold the GLSLC outputs captured on the Thor with `DM_GLSLC_CAPTURE` (`hle_glslc.c`) and split with `shader_split.py`: 31 shaders, 23 pixel and 8 vertex, 8 variation pairs. They cover the title, the menus and the demo's opening areas (about 20 minutes of play over several runs); 2 were compiled at boot, the rest at scene changes. A player's Build runs the game once to capture the table's shaders on the phone (`dev.peanuts.build.RuntimeShaders`, as for Fire Emblem: Three Houses). A later scene may compile a shader the table lacks: the host skips its draws (`[RED] vk: shader … is not in the shader library`) until a capture run with `DM_GLSLC_CAPTURE` and a new split add it.

## Found on this game

- **Fixed (host): other pools' textures.** The game keeps hundreds of small texture and sampler pools live (272 and 257 entries: NVN's 256 reserved descriptors, then 16 or 1 of its own) and keeps making new ones over fresh memory without finalizing the old. With the host's 16 pool ranges every pool past the 16th read the first pool's numbering: button icons over the title screen, scene art in the dialogue box, the HUD drawn from the font page, and `[RED] texture/sampler pools exceed 16` about 290,000 times in 90 s. Fix and numbers: [rendering.md](../../../docs/reference/rendering.md#many-small-descriptor-pools-read-another-pools-textures). After 15 minutes of play, 512 texture and 256 sampler pools were live.
- **A run before the capture draws nothing.** With an empty library (`--no-shaders` pack) the game boots and plays sound at 60 fps behind a black screen (100 % of draws skipped), which is how the capture run looks.
