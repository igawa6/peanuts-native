# Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter (`01002C9022770000`)

<!-- PEANUTS -->
The profile targets Switch updates **1.0.7 (`v393216`)** and **1.0.8 (`v458752`)**: SDK 21.4.4, NVN, and the build IDs in [game.tsv](game.tsv); the two share subsdk0 and sdk, only main differs. The lookup tables identify 6,605 shader keys, 6,602 for each update; they contain no shader programs. Prepare uses the player's own dump. Compared with 1.0.4, only `chr_cloth` shaders changed (six keys replaced by eight). 1.0.8 changes the volume fog compute shader and two `post_deferred` pixel shaders; the map tags each update's own three keys with its version, so Prepare of the other update does not expect them.

1.0.8's tables come from a full split of its RomFS and main. Its NPDM, the allocator's `0x48200000` block size (at `main+0x56ced4`) and the shadow stitch key are unchanged from 1.0.7. 1.0.8 was played on an AYN Odin 2 (Adreno 740) with Turnip v27.0.0-R1. Prepare ran on the handheld: 6,602 shaders in 5 min 4 s. The title screen reads Ver.1.08, a save written by an earlier update loaded in the City of Rolent, and about five minutes of field play through the streets, Melders' Orbal Factory and Elger's weapon shop held 60 FPS. A save made in 1.0.8 loaded again from the title screen. No battle was reached. The other Android results below are 1.0.7's.

Startup requests the application pool rounded down to 2 MiB, less `0x48200000`, for the heap. The `0xCD500000` pool bounds that request at `0x85200000`. Independently, the allocator requests `10 × 0x48200000` bytes of virtual address space; 1.0.7 sets that `0x48200000` block size at `main+0x56c0d4`, and its NPDM matches the base game's. The profile leaves 16 GiB for alias mappings. These are reservation sizes, not physical RAM measurements.

The first chapter's pixel key `e356305a9fe7e4de` identifies the same shadow-depth stitch as the second chapter's `eb824d256484ca7f`. On Turnip, the existing D16 depth-only pass split now recognizes either key; it does not change other drivers or apply the second chapter's frame pacing settings. Check for single-frame darkening of the road on Elize Highway after New Game. Gameplay, the original camera view and rendering on the system driver have not been verified on this branch.

## Android on the AYN Thor

Tested with update 1.0.7 and its 29 add-ons on an AYN Thor (Adreno 740) with Turnip v26.2.0-R5. New Game on Normal reached player control in the Bright Family House, the move outdoors and Elize Highway; a manual save was written and loaded again after a restart. The game held 60 FPS in the field at 8 to 11 ms of GPU time. In 1,316 recorded frames on Elize Highway no single frame darkened the road.

Frame rate: the game steps by elapsed time. A two-second walk ends at the same place at the game's own 60 FPS and at a cap of 30 (held exactly; a cap of 20 also held exactly, its walk was not measured), so Game settings offers caps up to 60 as experimental display pacing. The game limits itself to 60; the FPSLocker Warehouse patch for this build (`8963A531FA7F27A5`) changes a minimum frame delta and a resolution scale that the host rows cannot yet write, so 90 and 120 are not offered.

First launch: `vk_prewarm.bin` holds the pipeline keys of a Turnip run. With it a cold start prepares the shader parts of 1,271 programs in about 8 seconds, and the first scenes draw without pipelines made late; the next start takes about 1 second.

System driver: with 1.0.7 the stock Qualcomm driver starts the game and draws the title screen, menus and HUD, but the 3D field stays black, also on a second start. With 1.0.8 on an AYN Odin 2 (Qualcomm 512.676) the title screen and a save load, then the GPU hangs a few seconds into the field, in 2 of 2 runs (`vk: GPU hang: no progress; fence ... not reached in 6.1 s`). Use Turnip.

## Full resolution 3D (game option)

The game draws its 3D scene with a dynamic resolution: it moves between a floor and a 1600x900 maximum and scales the result to its 1920x1080 output. Under Peanuts Native it stays at the floor, 800x450 docked, even with GPU time to spare: on an AYN Odin 2 (Adreno 740, Turnip 27.0.0-R2) the field ran at 60 fps with the GPU busy 7.7 ms a frame while the scene pass drew an 800x450 viewport in its 1600x900 targets (`DM_VK_RT_LOG=1600x900`). Characters and ground look soft, and Resolution scale does not change it, since the game draws into a part of its targets.

The function at `main+0x8540` sets up the resolution and stores the floor from a table by console mode at `main+0x87a4` and `main+0x87a8`. The option **Full resolution 3D** (`DM_SKY1_FULL_RES`) replaces the two instructions that read that table (`main+0x8794`, `main+0x879c`) with loads of the maximum, so floor and maximum are both 1600x900 and the scene is always drawn at 1600x900. The code is the same in 1.0.7 and 1.0.8 apart from addresses outside these instructions; the rows are pinned to each update's main build ID.

Measured with Turnip 27.0.0-R2, docked, Resolution scale 1x. On an AYN Odin 2 (Adreno 740) with update 1.0.7, the option made the scene pass draw a 1600x900 viewport; the field held 60 fps (SurfaceFlinger, every frame 16.7 ms) with the GPU busy 13.4 ms, and in about 44 minutes of play through Rolent and the first dungeon 94 % of the 5 second pace windows were at 59.5 fps or more, while battles with large effects dropped a frame now and then (33 to 50 ms). On an AYN Thor (Adreno 740) with 1.0.7 and 1.0.8, the scene pass drew 800x450 without the option and 1600x900 with it, at the 60 fps pacing of its 117.9 Hz panel (58.9 fps), with the GPU busy 14.0 ms in Rolent (1.0.7) and 11.6 ms in the Rolent Sewers (1.0.8); about 7 minutes of 1.0.8 in the sewers (field, the treasure chest and its scene) stayed at 57.8 fps or more in every 5 second pace window. It is off by default because a slower GPU may not hold 60 at 1600x900.
