# Quick start

Five steps from nothing to playing. Each step links to the page with the details. Words in **bold** are buttons or menu names in the app. Words you may not know are in the [Glossary](Glossary).

## Before you start (2 minutes)

- Your phone has **Android 13 or newer**, a **Snapdragon** chip and **12 GB of RAM or more** (16 GB for Mario Kart World). Other phones may not work: look in [Tested devices](Tested-Devices) first.
- You have a copy of your game ("dump") that you made from **your own console**. This wiki does not explain how to get games and the app needs no keys.
- You have a few GB free on the phone and a charger. The first setup of a game takes a while.

## The steps

1. **Get the app.** Download the newest APK from the [releases page](https://git.eden-emu.dev/moceure/BananaNative/releases) (only from there) and install it. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser: that is normal. More: [Installation](Installation#get-the-app).
2. **Copy your game to the phone.** Put the game's folder (or its `.nup` file) anywhere you like, for example in a folder called `Switch2`. More: [Installation](Installation#what-you-need) shows how the folder should look.
3. **Allow file access.** Open **Banana Native**. If Home shows **Allow file access**, tap **Allow** and switch on **All files access** for the app, then go back. More: [Installation](Installation#allow-file-access).
4. **Add and prepare the game.** Tap **Add game**, pick your folder or `.nup` file, then tap **Prepare** when the app asks. This takes from 20 minutes to over an hour, once per game. Keep the phone on the charger. A Windows PC or a Mac does it faster ([PC or Mac](First-Start-and-Prepare#preparing-on-a-pc-or-a-mac)). More: [First start and Prepare](First-Start-and-Prepare).
5. **Play.** When the game's card says it is ready, tap **Play**. More: [Playing](Playing).

## If something looks wrong

- The app has a self-check: **Settings** > **Readiness check** says whether the phone can run your games and has buttons that fix what is missing.
- Low frame rate or heat: [Getting higher FPS](Getting-Higher-FPS) and [Heat and battery](Heat-and-Battery).
- A crash or a black screen: [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting). If that does not help, [open an issue](https://git.eden-emu.dev/moceure/BananaNative/issues) and attach the log.
